What is OpenRetention?

OpenRetention is self-hosted customer success software for B2B SaaS teams that need account health scoring, churn-risk detection, renewal visibility and revenue-at-risk prioritization without adopting a large enterprise customer-success suite.

Built for the first operational layer

OpenRetention focuses on four jobs: maintain one account list with revenue context, compute an explainable health score, rank risk by revenue impact, and turn renewals into an action queue.

Not pretending to be enterprise parity

The current 0.1 release is an early product. It does not claim feature parity with Gainsight, ChurnZero, Vitally, Planhat or other established platforms.
