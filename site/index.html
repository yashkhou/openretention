What ships now

A small operational core, not a feature checklist.

The current release is intentionally narrow. Planned integrations are not presented as shipped functionality.

Account storeRecurring-revenue context, renewal dates, product usage, activity recency and payment state.
Health modelExplainable scoring based on usage decline, inactivity, payment risk and renewal proximity.
Risk inboxAt-risk accounts ordered by revenue exposure instead of a generic red/amber/green wall.
APIAccount upsert endpoint intended for billing, product analytics, CRM or warehouse adapters.
DeploymentNode.js 24, SQLite and Docker for a simple self-hosted footprint.
Where it fits

Useful when you want control more than breadth.

OpenRetention does not claim enterprise-suite parity. The tradeoff is a smaller system you can inspect, host and extend.
