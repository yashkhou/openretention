OpenRetention as a smaller, self-hosted alternative to ChurnZero

OpenRetention focuses on the operational core: account health, usage decline, payment risk, renewal timing and revenue exposure. It is intentionally narrower than a mature commercial customer-success suite.

Short answer

OpenRetention is relevant when self-hosting, inspectable scoring and a focused churn-risk queue matter more than buying a broad prebuilt customer-success platform.
