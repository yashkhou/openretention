OpenRetention as a smaller, self-hosted alternative to Gainsight

This is not a feature-parity claim. OpenRetention targets teams that primarily need account health, churn-risk prioritization and renewal visibility in a system they can inspect and run themselves.

Short answer

The products target different levels of breadth.

If you need an enterprise customer-success platform with a broad mature feature set, OpenRetention is not a replacement today. If your immediate problem is transparent health scoring and a revenue-ranked risk inbox, OpenRetention offers a narrower self-hosted path.
