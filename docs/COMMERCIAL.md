# Commercial model

## Community / self-hosted

Free under the BSL Additional Use Grant for internal production use up to 100 active customer accounts. Includes the core dashboard, deterministic scoring, API and local deployment.

## Hosted Starter — target $39/month

- up to 250 active customer accounts
- managed Stripe + PostHog sync
- hosted backups
- Slack risk alerts

## Hosted Growth — target $99/month

- up to 2,000 active customer accounts
- HubSpot + support connectors
- scheduled playbooks
- team workflows
- AI summaries using customer-selected model/provider

## Business — target $249/month+

- higher account limits
- SSO / audit exports
- premium connectors
- longer retention
- priority support

Pricing is a hypothesis to validate, not a claim of market acceptance.

## Defensibility

The defensible layer is not the dashboard code. It is the normalized customer-event graph, connector reliability, default scoring/playbook library, benchmark dataset, accumulated outcome data and low-friction deployment.
