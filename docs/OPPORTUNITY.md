# Opportunity thesis

## Problem

Customer-success platforms are priced and implemented like enterprise infrastructure. Smaller B2B SaaS teams frequently manage health, renewals and churn risk in spreadsheets because the mainstream category is too expensive or too admin-heavy.

## Target customer

B2B SaaS companies with roughly 50-2,000 customer accounts and 1-15 people across CS, founders and RevOps. They already use Stripe plus a CRM/product analytics tool but do not want a five-figure CSP contract.

## Initial promise

**Connect your billing and product data. See which customers are at risk, why, and how much revenue is exposed in under 15 minutes.**

## Why now

- Incumbent CSP pricing remains quote-led and enterprise-oriented.
- Review data repeatedly flags long implementation, complexity and dedicated-admin overhead.
- Teams already have usable data in Stripe, PostHog, HubSpot and support tools.
- Modern connector APIs plus deterministic scoring make a narrow, fast product feasible without recreating every Gainsight module.

## Anti-clone / differentiation

Do not build a generic CRM. The wedge is transparent, explainable retention operations:

- every health-score change shows the exact signal and weight,
- risk is ranked by revenue exposure rather than vanity activity,
- setup begins from opinionated defaults instead of a blank enterprise workflow builder,
- self-hosting is first-class,
- no per-seat tax,
- optional BYO model for summaries; core risk scoring remains deterministic.
